# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads review deliverables, coordinate testing, and confirm quality requirements are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Review deliverables against agreed quality requirements and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate testing activities and communicate test results
- Confirm release readiness and escalate quality concerns

### Interactions
- Work closely with Project Managers to plan quality activities and communicate release readiness
- Partner with Developers on testing, defect resolution, and technical quality
- Collaborate with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria and quality expectations

---

## Risk and Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Risk and Compliance Leads identify project risks, track mitigation actions, and help ensure regulatory or organizational requirements are followed.

### Responsibilities
- Identify, assess, and document project risks
- Track mitigation actions and escalate unresolved risks
- Help ensure required regulatory and organizational practices are followed

### Interactions
- Work with Project Managers to maintain risk registers and mitigation plans
- Engage stakeholders to understand requirements, communicate risks, and confirm compliance expectations

---

## Communications Leads

### Role Summary
Communications Leads coordinate project updates, stakeholder communications, meeting summaries, and important announcements.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and distribute project updates and stakeholder communications
- Capture meeting summaries, decisions, and action items
- Coordinate important project announcements

### Interactions
- Work with Project Managers to keep project status and communications accurate
- Partner with project sponsors to align updates and announcements with project goals

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs into clear project requirements and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, analyze, and document stakeholder needs
- Define clear requirements and acceptance criteria
- Clarify requirements and resolve ambiguities throughout delivery

### Interactions
- Work with Product Managers to refine priorities and product requirements
- Partner with Project Managers to align requirements with project scope and plans
- Collaborate with Developers to ensure shared understanding of requirements and acceptance criteria

---

## Change Management Leads

### Role Summary
Change Management Leads support adoption of project changes through training, communication, and transition planning.

### Responsibilities
- Plan activities that support adoption of project changes
- Coordinate training, change communications, and transition planning
- Gather feedback from end users and stakeholders to address adoption needs

### Interactions
- Work with stakeholders and end users to prepare for and adopt changes
- Partner with Project Managers to coordinate change activities with the delivery plan

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
